Hydra Team is a collaborative Minecraft team focused on Bedrock content, visual identity and gameplay systems. We are a team focused on MC content with a high quality artwork and a advanced script skills.

Our work ranges from survival expansions and equipment systems to custom models, textures and premium packs. We publish community releases through CurseForge, MCPEDL and MCMODELS.
